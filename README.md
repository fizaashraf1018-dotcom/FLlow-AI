🚀 Flow AI

AI-Powered Learning, Productivity & Collaboration Platform

Flow AI is an intelligent platform designed to bring education, productivity, and collaboration together in one place. It enables teachers to build digital classrooms, students to learn more effectively with AI assistance, and professionals to organize their work through smart task management.

The platform combines classroom management, AI-powered learning tools, file sharing, quizzes, assignments, announcements, calendars, and productivity features into a modern and user-friendly experience.
